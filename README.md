# Spray Foam Insulation Cost Register (US, 2026)

Compiled by **Spray Foam Guide** (sprayfoamguide.com). Version 2026-10-21. **Draft — not yet published.**

A register of individual prices that US contractors and consumer cost guides publish on public web pages for open-cell and closed-cell spray foam insulation (installed and DIY). Each row is one published figure with its unit, scope, geography, publisher, source URL, source date, retrieval date and a short verbatim quote of the figure.

- **File:** `spray-foam-insulation-cost-register.csv` (44 rows)
- **Coverage:** 44 rows from 12 source URLs and 11 independent businesses; 2 primary-tier rows and 42 secondary-tier rows; local rows cover GA, MN, TX.
- **Build:** `python3 src/build.py` re-verifies every quote against the raw snapshots and fails if any quote or number is missing.
- **Docs:** METHODS.md, DATA-DICTIONARY.md, FINDINGS.md, raw/SOURCES.md
- **License:** CC BY 4.0 for the compilation (see LICENSE). Underlying figures belong to their publishers and are cited, not relicensed.

Not a quote, survey or representative sample. Do not average rows across different units, scopes or geographies.
